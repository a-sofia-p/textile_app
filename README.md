Inspiration for this project lies approximately 8,000 km (5,000 miles) from New York City, in one of the oldest and driest landscapes on Earth: the **Atacama Desert in Chile**. For over a decade, mounds of discarded clothing, accessories, and textiles have been accumulating at an accelerating pace, as global trade markets have been saturated by the relentless speed of fast fashion cycles. In 2024, Chile imported more than 60,000 tons of worn clothing and other articles, from the United States alone.

The **Cemetery of Clothes**, as referred to by the locals, serves as a vibrant wake-up call for stricter environmental protection policies and the urgent need for waste management solutions.

Leveraging data form the <a href="https://www.trademap.org/Country_SelProduct_TS.aspx?nvpm=1%7c%7c%7c%7c%7c6309%7c%7c%7c4%7c1%7c1%7c2%7c2%7c1%7c2%7c1%7c%7c1">International Trade Center (2015 - 2024)</a>, this project visualizes global worn clothing exports and the network between the top five exporting countries and their destinations.
